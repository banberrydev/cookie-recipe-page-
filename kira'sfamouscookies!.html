Ingredients

  1. 1 cup (227g) unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  2. 1¼ cups (250g) brown sugar
  3. ½ cup (100g) granulated sugar
  4. 1 large egg + 1 egg yolk
  5. 2 tsp vanilla extract
  6. 2½ cups (315g) all-purpose flour
  7. 1 tsp baking soda
  8. 1 tsp cornstarch
  9. ¾ tsp fine salt
  10. 2 cups (340g) semisweet chocolate chips or chunks
  11. Flaky sea salt for topping (optional but highly recommended)

The Secret Steps

  1. Melt the butter and let it cool for 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk butter, brown sugar, and white sugar until smooth.
  3. Add egg, yolk, and vanilla.
  4. Stir in flour, baking soda, cornstarch, and salt until just combined.
  5. Fold in chocolate.
  6. Cover and chill the dough for at least 2 hours (overnight is even better).
  7. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 11–13 minutes. The centers should still look slightly underdone.
  8. Immediately sprinkle with flaky salt.
  9. Cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes before moving.

Tips for Success

  • Chill the dough for at least 2 hours, or overnight for the best flavor and texture.
  • Use high-quality chocolate for rich, bakery-style cookies.
  • Do not overmix the dough after adding the flour; mix just until combined.
  • Bake until the edges are golden and the centers still look slightly underbaked.
  • Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes to finish setting.
  • Measure the flour carefully to avoid dry, dense cookies.

If I had to pick only 3 elite tips, they'd be:

  1. Chill the dough overnight.
  2. Slightly underbake the cookies.
  3. Use high-quality chocolate.
